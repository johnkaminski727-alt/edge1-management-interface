Shared rules

The channels can converge without sharing dangerous authority.

Read does not mean writeAI context never grants send, call, routing, or configuration authority.
Draft does not mean sendA prepared message remains a reviewable draft until a separate delivery gate is satisfied.
Content is untrustedMail, news, SMS/MMS, and telephony-derived content cannot grant scopes or change system policy.
Production traffic stays explicitCalls, messages, email, carrier actions, and emergency-path changes remain separate authorization boundaries.
