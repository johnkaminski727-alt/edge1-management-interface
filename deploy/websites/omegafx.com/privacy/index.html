PRIVACY

How OmegaFX Marketing Group Inc. handles information on this website and in its email correspondence.

Who we are

OmegaFX.com is operated by OmegaFX Marketing Group Inc., PO Box 333, Invermay, Saskatchewan S0A 1M0, Canada. Questions about this notice can be sent to contact@omegafx.com.

This website

OmegaFX.com is an informational page. It has no accounts, forms, advertising, third-party analytics or tracking cookies. Like any website, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested, the time and security events. These logs are used only to operate and secure the site and are kept for the hosting provider’s normal log-retention period.

Email correspondence

When you email OmegaFX or we email you, we use the message and the contact details in it to respond, keep business records and protect our systems. Incoming and outgoing mail is checked for spam, malware and impersonation. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive identification by ordinary email.

Correspondence records

Our outgoing messages carry a correspondence control number and a link you can use to view the record of that correspondence or acknowledge receipt. If you open that link, we may record the time, IP address and browser used, for security, delivery verification, records management and dispute resolution. We do not use hidden tracking pixels or device fingerprinting.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our website and communications (such as hosting, email and security providers), with professional advisers, or where the law requires it. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct personal information we hold about you, or ask questions about how it is handled, by writing to contact@omegafx.com or to the mailing address above. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

OmegaFX.com does not offer or solicit financial, brokerage, trading, advisory, investment, payment, remittance, cryptocurrency or foreign-exchange services through this website.
